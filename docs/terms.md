## Accuracy

Results come from third-party sources at the time of the run and can be incomplete, delayed or wrong. In particular:

- Lighthouse scores are lab measurements under simulated conditions and vary between runs; they are not real-user field data.
- "Available" means a registry has no registration; a registrar may still refuse the name or charge a premium price.
- Texas business data is the Texas Comptroller's "Active Franchise Tax Permit Holders" dataset, not the Secretary of State register, and the New York and Oregon datasets list active entities only.
- A sitemap lists what a site chooses to publish, not every page.

Do not rely on the results as legal, financial or compliance advice. Check important facts at the official source; each business record includes a `sourceUrl` for that.
